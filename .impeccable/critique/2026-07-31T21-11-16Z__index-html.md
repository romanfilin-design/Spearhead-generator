## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Authored for this product, not generic. Roman-numeral section markers, hero portraits with gradient scrims, phase-activation row (M/S/C/X), HP bar's steel→amber→red escalation, twist-card briefing reveal — all purpose-built for a grimdark combat tracker. Ember Red token discipline (aliased, not redefined) is real. Where it slips: round/VP/room controls are near-duplicated across every tab, and the two-player data model itself is inconsistent (see P0 below) — the skin is specific, the architecture underneath it is not fully thought through for the stated two-device parity principle.

**Deterministic scan**: Static CLI scan (`detect.mjs`) on index.html: exit 2, 16 findings — 14 `overused-font` (all the known Cinzel/Inter false positive per project ignore.md, filtered), 1 `em-dash-overuse` (advisory, 11 instances), 1 `dark-glow` (zero-offset box-shadow, line 237). Live DOM scans (mode picker, Prep, Twist, Army, Hand tabs) surfaced rule families the static scanner structurally cannot see: `undersized-ui-text` (multiple 9-10px functional labels), `low-contrast` (3.9:1 and 4.1-4.2:1 text, below the 4.5:1 floor — `#847a66` on `#241e17`/`#1c1712`, `#1a1305` on `#c25538`), `all-caps-body`, `text-occlusion` (locked mode-picker card's "Скоро" fully covered by its own name label), `nested-cards` (card-in-card on `.abilitycard`, 8 occurrences), and repeated `dark-glow` instances on active/selected states the static scan misses entirely. Live em-dash count roughly doubled once real army data loaded (22 vs. 11). No false positives found beyond the pre-filed overused-font entry.

**Visual overlays**: Not left running — the detector's live-server + injection process was stopped after each view per protocol; no persistent [Human] tab overlay remains for you to look at right now. Ask and it can be re-run and left open.

## Overall Impression

Strong, specific visual language sitting on top of an architecture that doesn't fully deliver its own stated promise. The Ember Red "Command Deck" identity is genuinely good and disciplined. But the app's biggest functional gap — Player 2 has no Tactics Hand at all — plus systemic undersized/low-contrast text directly undercut the two product principles that matter most here: two-device parity and dim-room legibility. Fix the structural and legibility issues; the decorative layer is mostly already earning its keep.

## What's Working

- **HP bar state escalation** (`.hpbar .fill.warn/.crit`) with a deliberate `prefers-reduced-motion` carve-out that keeps this specific color transition alive — correctly distinguishes decorative motion from state-carrying motion at the exact moment (a unit dying) it matters.
- **Toast-with-undo** (`toast(msg, undoFn)`) applied consistently across VP, HP, army reset, and turn-flag reset — turns the biggest risk of a fast one-handed tracker (mis-tap in a dim room) into a recoverable action instead of silent data loss.
- **Weapon-term glossary** (`TERM_GLOSSARY`/`term-clickable`) — real progressive disclosure, answers "what does Anti-X mean" in one tap without duplicating rules text in every row.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Player 2 has no Tactics Hand UI at all**
Why it matters: `#tab-hand` only renders `#handzone-p1` — zero `#handzone-p2` or equivalent anywhere in the file. The armybar explicitly supports two players sharing one device (И1/И2, no room needed), but on that same screen Player 2 can't track a hidden hand unless a second device joins a Firebase room, and nothing tells the user that. Direct violation of stated principle #3 (two-device parity).
Fix: Add a P1/P2 toggle to Тактики mirroring the armybar's dual-player pattern, or show an explicit notice when no room is active.
Suggested command: `/impeccable shape` (this is a missing feature/IA gap, not a visual fix)

**[P0] Undersized, low-contrast functional text against the stated primary accessibility concern**
Why it matters: `.armybar-vplabel` (9px), `.underdogtag` (10px), `.section-num` (10px), `.playerhead-label`/`.deckcount` (10-11.5px), `.lbl`/`.lbl2` (10px), `button.tiny` (10px) — confirmed live plus detector-flagged `low-contrast` at 3.9:1 and 4.1-4.2:1 (below WCAG's 4.5:1 floor). These are exactly the glanced-at-mid-game labels (round, whose turn, score gap) that must read at a glance in poor light, one-handed. This is the app's own declared #1 accessibility axis, failing on its own terms.
Fix: Raise functional/status text to ~12px minimum; push flagged text colors to at least 4.5:1 against their backgrounds.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit` then `/impeccable typeset`

**[P1] Mode picker's "more modes coming" extension point is nearly invisible**
Why it matters: `.poster.p2`/`.poster.p3` are reduced to a barely-visible sliver behind the selected card at mobile width; detector confirmed `text-occlusion` (locked poster's "Скоро" label fully covered by its own name label). Undercuts stated principle #4 — the mode picker is meant to read as a real extension point, not cosmetic, but a first-time user can't perceive it.
Fix: Increase visible offset/opacity step between stacked posters, or narrow p1 so the fan-out is legible at mobile widths; fix the text-occlusion on the locked label.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P2] Two incompatible multiplayer mental models coexist unreconciled**
Why it matters: Armybar assumes one shared device passed hand-to-hand (both players edit round/VP directly). Army tab assumes separate devices (opponent visible only read-only via Firebase room). Tactics tab is P1-only regardless of mode. A group could reasonably expect one phone to run the whole game and only discover mid-session that Army/Tactics silently don't support that.
Fix: Pick one model explicitly and build it out fully, or clearly label/gate the shared-device-only parts.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P3] Dead CSS: `.stickybar`/`.stickyrow` defined but unused**
Why it matters: Low severity, but leftover from an incomplete mockup port; maintenance trap for the next edit. Real tab bar uses `position:fixed; bottom:0`, unrelated to this class.
Fix: Remove, or wire up if a sticky top bar was actually intended.
Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (accessibility-dependent — dim-room legibility is the operative axis here)**: `.armybar-vplabel` at 9px carries "И1"/"И2" — read every single round. `.section-num` roman numerals (10px) plus a glow softens edges further, exactly wrong for low-light crispness. `.underdogtag` (10px, faint weight) carries the stat players care most about — who's behind — yet is the least emphasized element on the bar.

**Casey (distracted, one-handed at the table)**: `.roundctl` buttons (32px) and `.armybar` +/- (20px) sit below the file's own declared 44px floor, yet VP gets tapped every round without close looking. `.cardtoggle-btn` sits top-left of a 400px-tall `.herowrap` — collapsing a resolved unit means the thumb travels back up past a card it just scrolled through. Weapon-glossary tap opens the same full-screen modal as regiment/enhancement selection — a quick "what's Anti-X" glance interrupts the whole screen mid-combat instead of a lightweight popover.

**Jordan (first-timer)**: Opens Тактики expecting to see the opponent's hand too and finds nothing — no toggle, no label, no explanation a second device+room code is required. The room block ("Комната: Создать / [код] / Войти") has zero copy on what a room does or what breaks without one.

## Minor Observations

- `loadArmies()` catch block surfaces a dev-facing error string ("откройте через локальный сервер") directly to players.
- `.buffpill` stacking only handles two pills before overlap.
- `wtable`'s ≤480px fixed-width column split (hand-tuned percentages) is brittle if column count ever changes.
- `renderOppUnitSummary` applies `pointer-events:none` inline instead of via a class, inconsistent with the file's otherwise class-driven approach.
- No card-level "dead" state — a 0-HP unit differs from a damaged one only by `.hpval2.dead` text color.
- No end-of-game/winner screen — round caps at 4 with just a toast; the actual peak-end moment of the whole session is never surfaced.
