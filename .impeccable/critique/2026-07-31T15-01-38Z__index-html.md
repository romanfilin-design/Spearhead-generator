## Overall Impression

The bones are genuinely good — real game data, a distinctive visual language, and the best single mechanic in the app (toast-with-undo on stat mutations) shows someone thought hard about the actual failure mode this tool needs to defend against: a mis-tap during a tense moment. But three gaps undercut a tool whose entire premise is "fast, reliable glance during a live game": tab-switching silently keeps a stale scroll position, the app opens on step III of a I→V flow with no explanation, and the two highest-blast-radius actions in the app (full army reset, turn-flag reset) are the only mutations with no undo at all.

## What's Working

1. **Toast-with-undo on every stat mutation** (`changeUnitHP`, `changeVP`) — a precise answer to "what if I fat-finger this during combat," which is the exact anxiety a live-table tool needs to defuse.
2. **The weapon-ability glossary is scoped to the actual data** (`TERM_GLOSSARY`) — only defines terms that really occur in `armies.json`, surfaced inline via `.term-clickable` rather than a separate reference screen.
3. **HP bar's steel→amber→red escalation is a deliberate state signal**, explicitly exempted from `prefers-reduced-motion` because it's a meaningful "unit is dying" cue, not decoration — a rare case of someone reasoning correctly about which motion to keep.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Tab switch doesn't reset scroll position**
Why it matters: `switchTab()` only toggles `.active` classes, never scrolls to top. Verified live — scrolling to y=525 on Army then switching to Подготовка leaves the user 525px into unrelated content. This is the core interaction of the whole game (constant mid-round tab-hopping), so it fires constantly.
Fix: add `window.scrollTo(0,0)` (or reset `.wrap.scrollTop`) inside `switchTab()`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P1] App boots into tab III (Армия), not the numbered first step**
Why it matters: `initApp()` ends with `switchTab('army')`, but the app's own numbering (I Подготовка → II Твист → III Армия → V Тактики) implies Подготовка comes first. A first-time player lands on III with no cue they skipped I–II.
Fix: open on `tab-prep` by default, or add a first-run nudge toward step I.
Suggested command: `/impeccable onboard`

**[P2] Destructive resets have no confirm and no undo**
Why it matters: "Сбросить армию" (wipes all HP/activation state for an army) and the turn-flag reset are plain `ghost small` buttons, visually identical in weight to routine secondary actions, at the bottom of a long scroll — exactly where a distracted one-handed tap could land by accident. Every other mutation in the app gets toast-undo; these two, the highest-blast-radius actions, get none.
Fix: extend the existing toast-undo pattern to these two actions, or add a confirm step.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Tactics hand cards have no expand affordance**
Why it matters: `.handcard` is tap-to-expand exactly like the accordions elsewhere in the app, but carries none of the `▾` chevron cue that trains the user on that pattern. Confirmed live — nothing on the collapsed card signals it's interactive.
Fix: add a chevron or "tap for details" cue consistent with the accordion pattern.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P3] Locked mode-picker posters are visually inert**
Why it matters: `.poster.p2/.p3` are meant to peek out behind the active poster, but render as a nearly-invisible sliver against the void background at both viewports — underserving the "more modes coming" narrative.
Fix: bump the peeking posters' background lightness or add a visible border.
Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (distracted, one-handed, mobile)**: The scroll-reset bug (P1) is squarely a Casey problem — mid-game tab-hopping with a thumb, expecting the new tab to start at top, landing mid-content instead. Combined with no confirm on "Сбросить армию," a mis-tap while scrolling one-handed could wipe an entire army's HP tracking mid-game with zero recovery path.

**Sam (accessibility / keyboard / screen reader)**: Baseline keyboard support is solid — `:focus-visible` states defined on buttons, `aria-pressed` on phase toggles. But `.term-clickable` glossary spans use `onclick` + `tabindex="0"` with no `onkeydown`/`role="button"` — focusable but not keyboard-activatable. The one interactive control found that's reachable by Tab but can't be triggered without a mouse.

**Jordan (first-timer)**: Lands on tab III per the boot-order finding, with the app's own numbered-section convention (I–V) working against them since the app doesn't follow the order it implies.
